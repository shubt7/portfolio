About me

Analyst by training, translator by habit.

I am most useful where a business question, a data problem and a technical constraint all meet.

My work starts with listening: stakeholder interviews and working sessions that define what a report must answer, which rules apply, and what success looks like. From there I write the requirements and acceptance criteria, map data from source to target, and build the dashboards.

When numbers disagree, I trace them back to the source, find the root cause and work with engineering to fix it. Before anything goes live I check it against requirements in UAT, and then I present findings and risks to both technical and executive audiences.
